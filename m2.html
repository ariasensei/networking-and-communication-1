Twisted Pair Cable

Twisted pair is one of the most commonly used media in LANs.

Inside the cable are 8 conductors arranged into 4 twisted pairs.

Pair 1Pair 2Pair 3Pair 4

Why twist the wires?

Twisting helps reduce:

  • Electromagnetic interference (EMI)
  • Crosstalk
The twists help protect signal quality.

Fiber-Optic Cable

Fiber-optic cable carries information through light pulses instead of electrical signals.

💡 ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ✦ ➜ DATA

Advantages

  • Very high bandwidth
  • Long-distance transmission
  • Resistant to electromagnetic interference

Limitations

  • Specialized tools and skills
  • Installation may cost more than basic copper

Crosstalk

Crosstalk happens when signals from one wire interfere with another wire.

🗣️ “Hello!”     🗣️ “Good morning!”

It is similar to trying to listen to one conversation while another person is talking loudly beside you.

Twisting wire pairs helps reduce crosstalk.

Modern Networking Note: Auto-MDI/MDIX

Many modern Ethernet devices support Auto-MDI/MDIX.

Older Equipment

Cable type often had to match the traditional device-pairing rule.

Modern Equipment

The device may automatically adjust transmit and receive pairs.

You should still know both cable types because wiring standards remain fundamental networking skills.

Lab Safety and Good Practices

  • Handle cutters carefully and keep fingers away from blades.
  • Do not use damaged tools.
  • Check wire arrangement before crimping.

  • Keep pairs twisted as close to the termination as practical.
  • Test every completed cable.
  • Keep the work area clean.

Lesson Summary

Network media provide the pathway through which data travels.

  • Guided media: twisted pair, coaxial, and fiber optic.
  • Unguided media: wireless signals through air or space.
  • T568A and T568B define Ethernet conductor order.
  • Straight-through: same standard on both ends.
  • Crossover: T568A on one end and T568B on the other.
  • A networking student should be able to prepare, terminate, crimp, test, and troubleshoot Ethernet cables.
